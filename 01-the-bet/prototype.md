# The Prototype Bet

## What I Built
<!-- One sentence: what does this prototype demonstrate? --> An interactive prototype of the Michelin AI Assistant that demonstrates how conversational AI can become the primary interface for fleet managers to access operational intelligence. The prototype enables users to ask natural-language questions and receive trusted, evidence-backed recommendations supported by fleet data, insights and suggested next actions.

## AI Value Archetype
<!-- Automator / Copilot / Oracle / Creator / Orchestrator --> Operational Copilot — an AI assistant that augments fleet managers by interpreting proprietary operational data, prioritising what matters most, and accelerating trusted decision-making.

## The Bet in One Sentence
<!-- What you're building, for whom, why now --> If fleet managers can obtain trusted, evidence-backed operational answers through natural conversation faster than navigating dashboards and reports, they will increasingly rely on the AI Assistant as their primary workflow, improving engagement, retention and long-term customer value.

## Kill Criteria
<!-- When would you stop? What evidence would kill this bet? --> Within 6 months of General Availability, fewer than 30% of eligible customers use the AI Assistant weekly, or fewer than 50% of AI interactions result in a follow-up operational action, indicating that conversational AI is not becoming an indispensable workflow and the strategic investment should be reassessed.

# Pricing Strategy Block, Module 3

# Pricing Strategy
- Strategy posture: Penetrate

Rationale: Drive broad adoption of the AI Assistant and establish conversational fleet intelligence as a recurring operational workflow before optimising for direct AI monetisation. Maintain margin through aggressive optimisation of inference and infrastructure costs as usage scales.

- Pricing model: Hybrid (base + usage)
- Unit of work metered: Fleet insight delivered
- Base fee ($/month): 40
- Price per unit: $0.25
- Estimated units/user/month: 40
- Implied revenue/user/month: $50.00

Pricing principle: Price against the customer value delivered rather than underlying model consumption. Core access creates predictable recurring revenue, while metered pricing protects unit economics as AI usage grows.

# Decision Note
Why this pricing structure fits the buyer and the value delivered: 

We chose a penetration + hybrid pricing model because it matches how enterprise fleet customers buy and how the AI Assistant creates value. A predictable base fee makes adoption simple and keeps core AI capabilities accessible, while usage-based pricing allows heavier users to pay in proportion to the additional fleet insights and operational value they receive. This supports broad adoption without exposing MyCF to uncontrolled variable AI costs, while pricing the customer-facing outcome rather than underlying tokens or model calls.
